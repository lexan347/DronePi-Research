## Open decisions and evidence gaps

- Final chassis, flight-controller SKU, firmware and payload selection.
- Exact CAD export compatibility and available neutral CAD formats.
- Supported motor/propeller thrust and power curves, aircraft mass, center of gravity and inertias.
- Wiring completeness, connector pinouts, regulated power capacity and payload mounting.
- Full priced BOM including shipping, taxes, consumables and missing assembly items.
- Site access, survey requirements and operating permissions before field execution.
- Map documentation to existing workbook WBS IDs before reporting schedule variance.
